Use reproducible experiments. Compare equivalent task success, fidelity, isolation, versions, hardware, network/cache state, and waits. Preserve raw data and profiles. Report distributions, CPU, memory, cost, retries, crashes, and fallback. Do not optimize from intuition or publish a multiplier without fair evidence.
